Notes from building these

Snapping is the cheap half of the fix. Rounding the committed value to two significant figures is one line in toValue, and it is what makes 42 a number the game can hold at all. It does not make it easy to find: two significant figures over 5.7 decades is about 510 distinct values, so on a phone each one is still under a pixel of thumb travel. That is why the six differ in effort, not in whether they can hit.

The decimal point is the whole problem. Every concept that works splits magnitude from mantissa — a decade chip, a second slider, a wheel — because those are two different questions. "Roughly how big" is a gesture; "which number exactly" is a digit.

The ten-second clock is the constraint. A keypad is exact but costs a look down at your hands; a coarse-plus-fine slider keeps your eyes on the number. Test them with the timer, not by reading this.
